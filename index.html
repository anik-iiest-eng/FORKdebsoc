Why Debsoc? 

The Debating Society of IIEST, Shibpur, or DebSoc, is a student-led community built around conversation, critical thinking, and public speaking. It provides students a space to debate ideas, challenge perspectives, and develop confidence in expressing themselves.

What began as small weekly discussions grew into an active platform for debate and Model United Nations. DebSoc has hosted Oxford-style and parliamentary debates, public speaking sessions, memorial debates, and MUN conferences, while representing IIEST at institutions including IIT Kharagpur, IISER Kolkata, Jadavpur University, and IIT Guwahati.

Beyond competitions, DebSoc focuses on learning. Through regular sessions, practice debates, workshops, and peer mentorship, members develop stronger communication, critical thinking, and listening skills, regardless of prior experience.

The society's flagship events include BECON (Bengal Engineering Conclave of Nations), the Paramesh Ranjan Dhar Memorial Debate, Disputation, Voices in Focus, and collaborative events under Instruo and TechManthan. Even during the pandemic, DebSoc continued its debates, training sessions, and inductions online.

Today, DebSoc brings together students from diverse disciplines united by an interest in ideas, dialogue, and expression, offering a space to compete, learn, and grow.
